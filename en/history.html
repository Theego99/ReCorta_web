History

A record of the work, and what comes next.

From early research and field experience to founding ReCorta, here are the milestones behind our work.

  1. Oct 2024

    Research and development begins

    We began developing AI image and video analysis in response to the time required to review wildlife survey footage, drawing on field experience in Tsushima.

  2. Sep 28, 2025

    Featured on NHK’s “Darwin Has Come!”

    The image-analysis system we had developed was featured in a program about the Tsushima leopard cat.

  3. Sep 9, 2026

    ReCorta LLC incorporated

    ReCorta LLC was established with its registered office in Nishitokyo, Tokyo.

  4. Sep 11, 2026

    WildCatcher 2.2.0 released

    The first public releases for Windows and Mac improved in-app updates, analysis recovery and result review.

  5. Sep 18, 2026

    Japanese corporate number assigned

    ReCorta was assigned its Japanese corporate number.

  6. Oct 14, 2026

    WildCatcher 2.2.6 released

    The latest versions for Windows and macOS are available, with clearer update guidance and safer retries on Windows.
