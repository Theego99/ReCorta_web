01 / WILDLIFE ANALYSIS

Wildlife image and video analysis

Available · Windows / macOS

WildCatcher

Find animals, people and vehicles across large camera-trap collections. Review and correct results on your computer, then export the records you need.

  • Detect animals, people and vehicles in camera-trap images and video
  • Review and correct detections and species classifications on your computer
  • Export survey records in formats such as CSV and Excel

Some features, including species classification and PDF export, vary by licence.

02 / EIA TOOLKIT

Environmental impact assessment (EIA)

In development · pilot evaluation

Wildpass

We are building a workspace that connects EIA schedules, field records, source documents, team reviews and comments to editable Word reports.

  • Organize project stages, assignments and deadlines alongside field observations
  • Link photographs and source documents to each record and retain review history
  • Prepare editable Word reports and coordinate responses to comments

Wildpass is in pilot evaluation with practitioners. Availability and timing depend on the project and evaluation scope.

Wildpass supports professional judgment. It does not automatically determine legal compliance, species identity, impact assessments or regulatory submissions.
