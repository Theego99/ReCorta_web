ReCorta will attend the wildlife damage summit pre-event

ReCorta will attend the October 26 pre-event for the 14th National Wildlife Damage Countermeasures Summit at AP Tokyo Yaesu, where local governments and specialist organizations will discuss local challenges and practical collaboration.

ReCorta will attend the pre-event for the 14th National Wildlife Damage Countermeasures Summit on Monday, October 26, 2026, from 10:00 to 17:00 at AP Tokyo Yaesu. The program includes exchanges among local governments, presentations by specialist organizations and companies, and opportunities to discuss practical solutions.

We hope to learn more about the needs of local teams working on wildlife surveys and environmental assessment, and to apply those insights to the tools we are building. If you are attending, we would be glad to say hello.
