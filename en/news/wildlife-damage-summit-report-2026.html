ReCorta presents WildCatcher at the 14th National Wildlife Damage Countermeasures Summit pre-event

ReCorta exhibited WildCatcher at the summit pre-event held at AP Tokyo Yaesu on October 26, 2026.

On October 26, 2026, ReCorta took part in the pre-event for the 14th National Wildlife Damage Countermeasures Summit at AP Tokyo Yaesu. The program included a discussion of challenges faced by local governments, presentations by 21 companies and organizations, and an exhibition and exchange session with 26 exhibitors.

ReCorta joined the company presentation session and exhibited on the 11th floor, introducing WildCatcher. The demo and poster showed a PC-based camera-trap workflow that detects animals in images and video, classifies species, lets a person review and correct results, and exports data to formats such as CSV, Excel, and JSON. Survey footage can be processed on the PC without being uploaded to the cloud.

Thank you to everyone we spoke with and to the organizers who made the event possible. We will keep building practical tools for wildlife surveys and environmental fieldwork.
