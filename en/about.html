Our mission and company

ReCorta LLC develops software to support wildlife surveys and environmental impact assessment work.

Our direction

Mission and vision

Mission

We support people working to protect biodiversity by listening to those closest to the field and putting the knowledge and flexible ideas of people who love nature to work, while building relationships of mutual respect between people, nature, and AI.

Vision

A society where people, nature, and AI respect one another, and the people and communities connected to biodiversity can thrive together.
